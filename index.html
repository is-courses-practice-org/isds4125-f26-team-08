About the Shop

Red Stick Reader is an independent bookshop on 5120 Plank Road in Baton Rouge. We carry new and used books across two floors, and we will order anything we do not have on the shelf.

What We Sell

  • New fiction, non-fiction and poetry
  • A large used section, traded and bought daily
  • Louisiana and Gulf South writing, our deepest shelf
  • Children’s books and young adult
  • Gift cards, in any amount

Our Story

The shop opened in 1998 in what used to be a hardware store. The tin ceiling and the freight scale by the door are original. Everything else is books.

We host readings, four book clubs and an annual poetry night that has outgrown the building twice.
